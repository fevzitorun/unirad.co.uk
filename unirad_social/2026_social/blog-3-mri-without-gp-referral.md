**The short answer: yes.** In Scotland you can book most private MRI scans yourself, without visiting your GP first. This is called *self-referral*, and at Unirad Diagnostic Imaging in Glasgow it takes less than five minutes online.

## How Self-Referral Works

1. **Choose your scan** — select the body part (knee, spine, brain, shoulder and more) on our booking page.
2. **Complete a short safety questionnaire** — a few questions about implants, previous surgery and your symptoms.
3. **Clinical review** — every booking is checked by our clinical team before your appointment. If an MRI isn't the right test for your symptoms, we'll tell you — and you won't pay for a scan you don't need.
4. **Attend your appointment** — most patients are scanned within the same week, including Saturdays.

## Which Scans Can I Self-Refer For?

Most musculoskeletal and neurological scans accept self-referral, including:

- Knee, shoulder, hip, ankle and wrist MRI — from **£290**
- Spine MRI (cervical, thoracic, lumbar or whole spine)
- Brain MRI
- Multi-region scans (two regions £495, three £695, four £895)

A small number of specialised scans — such as Cardiac MRI and some full-body packages — require a short clinical callback before booking. Breast MRI and certain abdominal studies may need additional clinical information.

## What About the Report — Will My GP Accept It?

Every Unirad scan includes a **consultant radiologist report within 5 working days**, and our reports are accepted by all UK GPs and consultants. Many patients bring their report straight to their NHS GP to fast-track the next step of their care.

If you'd like help understanding your results, we also offer an **online GP consultation after your scan for £50**.

## Why Patients Choose Self-Referral

- **No waiting for a GP appointment** — NHS GP waits in Glasgow can be 2–3 weeks alone
- **No referral letter fee** from a private GP (£40+ elsewhere)
- **Same-week scans** — evenings and Saturdays available
- **Transparent pricing** — from £290, no hidden fees
