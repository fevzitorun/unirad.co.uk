## What Happens at a Private MRI Scan?

Here's what to expect when you come to Unirad:

1. **Before your appointment** — you'll complete a brief MRI safety checklist when booking online. This covers things like metal implants, previous surgeries, and any relevant medical history.
2. **Arrival** — check in at our Glasgow clinic at 22 Loanbank Quadrant, G51 3HZ. The team will confirm your details and answer any questions.
3. **The scan** — you'll lie in the MRI scanner for between 20 and 60 minutes depending on how many regions are being scanned. The machine is loud but painless — earplugs are provided.
4. **After your scan** — your images are reviewed by a UK consultant radiologist. A full written report is delivered to you securely within 72 hours, along with digital access to your images.
5. **Sharing your results** — you can share your report and images directly with your GP, specialist, or any other healthcare provider you choose.
