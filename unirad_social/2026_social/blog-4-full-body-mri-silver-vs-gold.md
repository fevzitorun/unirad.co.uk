## Who Is the Silver Package For?

The **Silver package (£640)** screens the brain, abdomen and pelvis — covering major organs including the liver, kidneys, pancreas, bladder and reproductive organs, plus the brain.

Choose Silver if you:

- Want a general health MOT without a specific symptom
- Are under 50 with no back or neck complaints
- Want the most affordable comprehensive screen

## Who Is the Gold Package For?

The **Gold package (£1,260)** adds a complete spine study — cervical, thoracic and lumbar — to everything in Silver.

Choose Gold if you:

- Have a history of back or neck pain, or a physically demanding job
- Spend long hours at a desk (disc problems are increasingly common from your 30s onwards)
- Want the most complete picture in a single visit

## What a Full Body MRI Can — and Can't — Do

We believe in honest medicine. A full body MRI is excellent at detecting structural changes: many tumours, cysts, disc degeneration, organ abnormalities and inflammation. It is **not** a substitute for cardiac screening (see our Platinum package, which adds cardiac MRI), blood tests, or cancer screening programmes you're invited to by the NHS.

Every scan is reported by a consultant radiologist, and if anything needs follow-up, your report gives your GP exactly what they need to act quickly.
