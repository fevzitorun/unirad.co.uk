## Is a Private MRI Scan Worth It?

For many patients, the answer comes down to two things: **time** and **certainty**.

If you're in pain and waiting 20 weeks for an NHS appointment is affecting your work, sleep, or quality of life, a private scan at £290 can deliver answers in days. That report can then be shared with your GP, a private consultant, or a physiotherapist — accelerating your treatment pathway regardless of whether you continue privately or return to NHS care.

Private MRI is particularly good value for:

- **Musculoskeletal injuries** — knee, shoulder, hip, spine — where early diagnosis leads to better outcomes
- **Neurological symptoms** — persistent headaches, dizziness, cognitive concerns
- **Preventive health screening** — full body packages for peace of mind
- **Avoiding NHS delays** — especially for non-urgent referrals with long wait times
